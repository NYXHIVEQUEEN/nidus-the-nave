## Faces

Queen's order on this pass: no old faces. The written ages of 40 and mid-40s were dropped. Everyone reads about 25. Hair colors that used to mean age (silver, white) are dye on a young face.

- vesper — Nordic, pale, ash-blonde braids, frost-grey eyes, young
- mora — East African, deep brown skin, shaved head, ember tattoos, young
- ashlar — South Asian, black bun, soot on one cheek, young
- narthex — silver hair as dye, lace beside the face, eyes clear, young (not a veiled elder)
- pyre — copper-red hair, freckles, hazel eyes, young
- sable — East Asian, sharp black bob, beauty mark, smirk, young
- dirge — crest pushed back so the face shows, scar on the chin, young
- reliquary — dark hair, candle crown, smooth face, no grey
- briar — red curls, green eyes, thorn tattoo on the neck, young
- nyxara — dark brown skin, high cheekbones, cathedral crown, young
- cinder — buzzed hair, bandana, soot, young
- brazier — auburn hair, visor up, burn scar, young
- spire — platinum crop, pale blue eyes, young
- veil — kohl-lined eyes, hood framing the face, young
- halo — big curly hair, choir collar, warm smile, young
- tithe — high black ponytail, sly grin, young
- thorn — undercut, scar through one eyebrow, fierce grin, young
- matins — West African, hood back, lantern on the face, young
- ossuary — bleached white hair, dark lips, living face, young
- abbess — stern young face, gilt circlet, not a wimple
