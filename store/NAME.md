# NIDUS name check (knockout, not a lawyer)

Keep **NIDUS** in the hive. On stores, prefer **NIDUS: The Nave** so it is not a bare one-word collision.

## What exists

- **Warframe — Nidus** (Digital Extremes). A playable frame, not a standalone game titled NIDUS. WARFRAME is the registered house mark. This is the real gamer-search collision. You are not cloning their infestation kit; still, do not use their logo, “Prime,” or Infested look.
- **Google Play “Nidus”** medical / clinic / insurance apps (Golden Skill, iSocial, Nidus Seguros). Different class. Low risk for a gothic idle.
- **Steam**: no live title that is just NIDUS as a game as of this check.
- Latin *nidus* = nest. Fits a hive. Weak as a trademark (descriptive-ish) but usable.

## Store fields

- App name: `NIDUS: The Nave` (30 chars)
- Short: `Gothic idle hive`
- Package: `com.nyxhivequeen.nidus` (fine)
- In-game title stays **NIDUS**. Subtitle **THE NAVE** is on the wake screen.

## If Play or a lawyer flinches

Backup titles that still sound like this hive: **THE NAVE**, **NAVE HIVE**, **NYX NIDUS**. Do not rename in code until she says so.

This is a search, not clearance. File USPTO class 9 + 41 if she wants the word locked.
